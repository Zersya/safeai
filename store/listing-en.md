# Play Store listing (en-US)

**Title (≤30):** safeAI: Safe Link Checker

**Short description (≤80):** Checks links from WhatsApp & SMS before they open. Protects parents from scams.

**Full description (≤4000):**

Scam links on WhatsApp and SMS are everywhere. One wrong tap can cost you passwords, PINs, or money. safeAI checks every link BEFORE it opens, with big, clear screens that are easy for parents to use.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Make safeAI your main browser app (one time, guided inside the app).
2. Tap a link in WhatsApp, SMS, or any other app. safeAI checks it first.
3. If it is safe, the link opens automatically in the browser of your choice (for example Chrome). If it is dangerous, it is blocked.

You can also use Share > safeAI from other apps, or paste a link on the home screen.

THREE WARNING LEVELS
• GREEN – No danger found. The link opens automatically after 2 seconds. A Cancel button is shown if you change your mind.
• YELLOW – Suspicious. For example an address that imitates a bank's name, a link that downloads an app (.apk), or a numeric address. The link opens only if you choose "Open anyway" and confirm.
• RED – Danger. Google Safe Browsing flags the site as deceptive or as containing harmful programs. The link is fully blocked, with no button to open it.

If a link cannot be checked (for example there is no internet), safeAI tells you and offers "Try again".

DESIGNED FOR PARENTS
• Large text, large buttons, clear colors with icons and words.
• Indonesian and English.
• A short guide the first time you open the app.

PRIVACY
• No ads. No accounts. No payments.
• Check history stays on your phone and can be deleted at any time.
• To check a link, only a short code (a 4-byte hash prefix) is sent to Google Safe Browsing, not the link itself.

IMPORTANT
Checks use Google Safe Browsing and on-device rules. Results cannot be guaranteed to be 100% accurate. Stay careful, and never give your password, PIN, or OTP code to anyone.
